👤 Applicant Lifecycle

  • Registration: Supabase Auth & email verification.
  • Profile & Assets: Profile details, skills & resume upload.
  • Applications: Job search & AI-assisted application tracking.
  • Compliance: Govt IDs, NBI clearance & medical upload.

🎯 Talent Acquisition (TA)

  • Job Postings: Create & publish job openings from MRFs.
  • Sourcing: Inbound review + KNN vector talent pool search.
  • Screening: AI score evaluation & initial interviews.
  • Endorsement: Client submissions & deployment activation.

⚙️ Administrator

  • Users & Roles: Staff accounts, permissions & access.
  • AI & Scoring: Configure weights & feature toggles.
  • Clients & MRFs: Register clients & approve requisitions.
  • Audit & KPIs: Immutable logs, hiring speed & reports.
